# Executive brief — Hannah's Bread Company
**Dynatrace® migration: cross-channel observability and mission-critical app monitoring**
Prepared by: Business Insights Analyst | 2026-05-28 | Confidential

---

## Problem summary

Hannah's Bread Company is transitioning to Dynatrace® from Datadog across 30 U.S. and Canada facilities. Today, the IT team learns of outages in mission-critical logistics applications — breadSHIP (distribution) and breadSAIL (yard management) — from facility staff and customers, not from monitoring alerts. A fast-growing mobile ordering app is delivering Hannah's promise to consumers directly, but has no performance visibility: every failed order, every slow checkout, and every geographic gap is invisible until a customer calls. With a national expansion on the horizon and no Service Level Objective (SLO) tracking in place, Hannah's is scaling without a measurable safety net.

---

## Business impact

- Fresh product delivery depends on breadSHIP and breadSAIL. When these apps slow down or fail, dock workers, yard coordinators, and drivers cannot complete time-sensitive dispatch operations — directly risking spoilage and missed delivery windows.
- The mobile ordering app is Hannah's fastest-growing direct revenue channel. Without performance instrumentation, mobile order failures accumulate silently until a customer complains.
- Every outage is currently discovered reactively. The Datadog synthetic inventory has not yet been confirmed and no Dynatrace Synthetic monitoring is configured — how long outages go undetected before the IT team responds is not yet measured.
- National expansion is planned with no monitoring readiness indicators. New facilities cannot be validated as operationally ready without established coverage benchmarks.

---

## Top findings

1. **Facility staff and customers discover outages before IT does.** No proactive Synthetic monitoring covers breadSHIP, breadSAIL, or the mobile app API. Datadog synthetic coverage scope requires confirmation before the full gap is known.

2. **Monitoring coverage across all 30 facilities is unconfirmed.** The baseline host inventory (IA-02) has not been completed — the full exposure of the infrastructure gap is unknown. Dynatrace has zero coverage today; Datadog scope is pending the inventory.

3. **Availability is a verbal commitment, not a measured target.** No SLO tracking exists in Dynatrace, and target values have not been formally agreed. SLO configuration requires a target-setting workshop as a prerequisite — not just a platform configuration task.

4. **Mobile order failures go undetected until a customer complains.** The mobile ordering app, Hannah's fastest-growing revenue channel, has no Real User Monitoring (RUM) or performance instrumentation across devices, browsers, or geographies.

5. **Leadership learns of operational impact from logistics reports, not monitoring dashboards.** No instrumented connection exists between application performance and business outcomes such as order delays, dispatch failures, or conversion rates.

---

## Recommended actions

**Wave 1 — Weeks 1–3 (low disruption; no host agents or code changes)**
- Deploy Dynatrace Synthetic monitors on all mission-critical endpoints — delivers proactive availability alerting and the red/green availability dashboard the technical team needs. Owner: Dynatrace consultant + Hannah's IT ops.
- Configure SLOs and availability dashboards using targets agreed in IA-03. Owner: Consultant + VP of IT. Risk: targets set too aggressively will trigger an immediate breach state — start at 99.0% and tighten after a 30-day baseline.
- Enable Web RUM to establish a baseline for visitor behavior and page performance. Owner: Consultant + named web team contact.

**Wave 2 — Weeks 3–8 (requires named technical DRI before starting)**
- Deploy OneAgent® on breadSHIP and breadSAIL — non-production pilot for 5 business days, then production with a documented rollback plan. Owner: Hannah's named technical DRI + consultant. Risk: Wave 2 cannot begin without a named DRI with production deployment authority. A production deployment on mission-critical logistics systems with no accountable internal owner is not acceptable.

**Wave 3 — Timeline TBD (begins after Wave 2 is stable in production)**
- Deploy Mobile RUM and configure Business Events for the mobile conversion funnel and logistics outcomes. Owner: Hannah's mobile engineering team + consultant. Timeline: TBD — contingent on IA-06 (mobile engineering contact and app release cadence confirmation). Do not commit this timeline to leadership until IA-06 is resolved.

## Decision asks

1. **VP of IT — Day 1:** Approve export of all Datadog monitoring data before the contract lapses. Without this data, the historical baseline for measuring the Dynatrace migration return on investment disappears permanently.

2. **VP of IT — Day 3:** Name a technical DRI with production deployment authority for breadSHIP and breadSAIL. Without a named DRI, Wave 2 cannot begin — and Waves 3 and 4 are also blocked.

3. **VP of IT + VP of Operations — Day 5:** Approve SLO targets for breadSHIP, breadSAIL, and the mobile app. This requires a target-setting workshop with application owners — not just a Dynatrace configuration task.

4. **CTO — Day 5:** Approve full Dynatrace implementation scope and confirm a mobile engineering contact. Wave 3 timing is TBD until this is resolved.

5. **VP of Operations — Day 5:** Provide the national expansion plan (facilities, timeline, geographies). Without this input, national expansion readiness cannot be instrumented and remains an unmeasured leadership KPI.
