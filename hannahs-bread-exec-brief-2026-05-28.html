Problem summary

The IT team learns of outages from staff and customers — not from monitoring

Hannah's is transitioning to Dynatrace® from Datadog across 30 U.S. and Canada facilities. Two mission-critical logistics applications — breadSHIP (distribution) and breadSAIL (yard management) — have significant performance issues with no Application Performance Monitoring (APM) visibility. A fast-growing mobile ordering app delivers Hannah's promise directly to consumers, but has no performance instrumentation: every failed order, every slow checkout, and every geographic gap is invisible until a customer calls. With national expansion planned and no Service Level Objective (SLO) tracking in place, Hannah's is scaling without a measurable safety net.

Business impact

Fresh product delivery, mobile revenue, and national expansion are all at risk

Operations: When breadSHIP or breadSAIL slows down or fails, dock workers, yard coordinators, and drivers cannot complete time-sensitive dispatch operations — directly risking spoilage and missed delivery windows.
Mobile revenue: The mobile ordering app is Hannah's fastest-growing direct revenue channel. Without performance instrumentation, mobile order failures accumulate silently until a customer calls.
Detection: Every outage is discovered reactively. How long problems go undetected before the IT team responds is currently not measured.
Expansion: National growth is planned with no monitoring readiness indicators — new facilities cannot be validated as operationally ready without established coverage benchmarks.

Recommended actions

Four sequenced implementation waves — serialized for a change-averse team

1
Foundation monitoring Weeks 1–3
Deploy Synthetic monitors on all mission-critical endpoints; configure SLOs and red/green availability dashboards; enable Web RUM for a visitor behavior baseline. No host agents or code changes required.
Risk: SLO targets set too aggressively trigger a breach state on Day 1 — start at 99.0%, tighten after 30 days.
2
Core APM — breadSHIP & breadSAIL Weeks 3–8
Deploy OneAgent® on breadSHIP and breadSAIL hosts. Non-production pilot for 5 business days; then production with a documented rollback plan. Validate host coverage across all 30 facilities against the inventory baseline.
Gate: Wave 2 requires a named technical DRI with production deployment authority. Without one, this wave does not start.
3
Mobile RUM & business intelligence TBD
Deploy Mobile RUM SDK into the mobile ordering app; configure Business Events for the conversion funnel and logistics outcomes. Timeline TBD — contingent on mobile engineering contact and app release cadence confirmation (IA-06). Begins only after Wave 2 is stable in production.
Risk: Do not commit this timeline to leadership until IA-06 is resolved.
