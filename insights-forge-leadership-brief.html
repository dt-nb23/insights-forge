The solution

Where rigor is encoded — not replaced

The GUIDE framework

G
Ground
Account maturity, vertical, consumption, AI adoption stage
U
Understand
Success criteria: renewal, expansion, exec motion
I
Illustrate
AI kicks in — selects the right framework, explains the why
D
Drive
Step sequencing, required artifacts, quality gates
E
Explain & evolve
Why this framework, what signals changed, what comes next

AI activates at Illustrate — choosing from proven frameworks, never inventing them. Humans own every decision through Drive and Evolve.

How users interact — 3 phases

Each phase pairs a user interaction with an AI response, anchored in GUIDE

1
Set context and success criteria
User shares account situation and desired outcomes; AI grounds the engagement in maturity, vertical, and goals — then maps to renewal, expansion, or exec motion
Maps to: G → U
2
Diagnose and present framework options Core
AI illustrates the right consulting framework, explains the rationale, and surfaces trade-offs and assumptions for IAT review and approval
Maps to: I — where AI kicks in
3
Deliver and evolve
AI drives execution discipline with artifacts and quality gates; consultant validates with the IAT and evolves the approach with the customer
Maps to: D → E

Design principles

Augments judgment — never replaces it

AI must do
✓Recommend the right success motion and surface proactive risk signals
✓Generate executive-ready narratives and data-backed value hypotheses
✓Structure technical discovery and next-best actions using clear frameworks
AI must not do
✗Replace human judgment, relationship ownership, or decision accountability
✗Commit to timelines, pricing, or scope, or act on systems autonomously
✗Provide generic recommendations without customer context or traceability
