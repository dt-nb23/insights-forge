The GUIDE framework

G
Ground
Account maturity, vertical, consumption, AI adoption stage
U
Understand
Success criteria: renewal, expansion, exec vs practitioner
I
Illustrate
AI chooses, not invents, the right consulting framework
D
Drive
Execution discipline: sequencing, required artifacts, quality gates
E
Explain & evolve
Why this framework, what signals changed, what comes next

AI activates at Illustrate — choosing from proven frameworks, never inventing them. Humans own every decision through Drive and Evolve.

How users interact — 3 phases

Each phase pairs a user interaction with an AI response, anchored in GUIDE

1
Context & Goals
User shares account context and desired outcomes; AI grounds the engagement in maturity, vertical, and goals — mapping to renewal, expansion, or exec audience
Grounds context, then understands success criteria
2
Diagnose & Recommend Core

Illustrates options — where AI activates
3
Execute & Deliver
AI drives execution discipline with artifacts and quality gates; consultant validates with the IAT and evolves the approach with the customer
Drives execution, then explains and evolves
Built-in quality gates

Six review passes stress-test every leadership-facing output — two structural checks and four perspective reviews

Structural checks — frameworks
MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive) — audits the issue tree for gaps and overlaps
ICE (Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort) — re-ranks priorities by expected value
Perspective reviews — personas
Optimist — surfaces upside and parallelization opportunities the team may have under-weighted
Consultative — translates findings into the firm’s leadership voice
Customer — checks alignment with real user experience and operational fit
Skeptic — prepares for hostile Q&A and stress-tests claims before leadership review
